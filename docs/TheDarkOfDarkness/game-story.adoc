== Story Premise

You enter a dark mansion while monsters roam outside. The adventure unfolds across the mansion grounds, upper levels, tower, and the dungeon under the shed.

== Backstory

The mansion's wizard was old and reckless, experimenting with forbidden dark magic. His spell spiraled out of control, summoning monsters that killed him, escaped the dungeon, and attacked the village.

The Dark Elf served the wizard and has failed to close the summoning portal or reverse the curse alone.

== Characters

=== Dark Elf (Apple Tree in Yard)

* Type: NPC (guide and quest-giver)
* Physical location: under the Apple Tree (which is inside the yard)
* Motivation: terrified of the monsters now roaming free, and specifically fearful of the Troll on the first floor
* Role in progression:
** helps the player understand the objective and sequence
** can repair the broken wand if the player brings all wand parts
** repaired output: _Dark Cursed wand_

=== Vegetable Spirit (Kitchen)

* Type: NPC (likely a cabbage spirit)
* Current story note: his mother was eaten by the wizard
* Visibility rule: appears only after the rabbit has been fed
* Narrative role: holds a missing scrap of paper needed to restore the spellbook

=== Rabbit (Kitchen)

* Type: NPC
* Gameplay role: gatekeeper for revealing the Vegetable Spirit
* Requirement: must be fed Rabbit food

=== Troll (First Floor)

* Type: NPC/monster threat
* Location: Ballroom
* Story impact: a major source of fear for the Dark Elf
* Defeat condition:
** place mirror shard #1 in sunlight in the Study
** place mirror shard #2 in reflected light at the Landing
** use the final shard in the Ballroom to bathe the Troll in sunlight, turning it to stone
* Fail condition: entering the Ballroom without the final shard and both placement steps completed gets the player torn apart by the Troll (_The End_; restart available)
* Drop: Wand tip (second half of the wand)

=== Vampire (First Floor)

* Type: NPC/monster threat
* Location: Bathroom
* Story detail: shattered mirrors because it could not see its reflection
* Defeat condition: stake it through the heart with the Wooden sword
* Fail condition: facing the vampire without the Wooden sword gets the player drained dry (_The End_; restart available)
* Drop: Wand handle (first half of the wand)

=== Werewolf (Tower)

* Type: NPC/monster threat
* Location: Tower chamber
* Defeat condition: must use the Loaded Gun
* Fail condition: entering the Tower chamber without the Loaded Gun gets the player mauled by the Werewolf (_The End_; restart available)
* Drop: Dark Mysterious obscure Key (used for shed/dungeon access)

== Location Story Notes

=== Kitchen

* Contains the Rabbit NPC.
* The Vegetable Spirit is only visible here after feeding the rabbit.
* The spirit holds the spellbook paper scrap.

=== Yard

* The Apple Tree is in the yard.
* The Dark Elf is specifically under the Apple Tree (not generally in the yard).
* Rabbit food is found on the ground in the yard.

=== Living Room

* Planned Toy Chest branch with childlike artifacts.

=== First Floor

* Bathroom: Vampire encounter and mirror shards.
* Library: contains a bookshelf location with a key crafting reference book.
* Study: mirror shard sunlight setup step, plus a small forge and bullet mold crafting station.
* Landing: mirror shard reflected-light setup step.
* Ballroom: Troll encounter; contains a display gun and silver cutlery.
* Crafting gate: bullet mold requires both melted silver from the forge and _Bullet Making for Dummies_ from the bookshelf.
* Global action: if player has both gun and silver bullets, they can combine them anywhere into a Loaded Gun.

=== Tower

* Contains the Werewolf encounter.
* Werewolf can only be defeated using the Loaded Gun.
* Tower chamber supports return command `leave` / `exit` back to the stairwell.
* Werewolf defeat drops the Dark Mysterious obscure Key.

=== Library Bookshelf (Funny Titles)

* _Bullet Making for Dummies_
* _Fifty Shades of Prey_
* _How to Win Fights with Interpretive Dance_
* _Necromancy for People Who Hate Mornings_
* _101 Uses for Haunted Cutlery_
* _The Complete Guide to Overthinking Traps_

=== Dungeon Under Shed

* Entry requires the Dark Mysterious obscure Key to open the shed.
* From the shed, an ominous dark stairway descends into the dungeon.
* The cells contain only fractured, meat-picked bones of the wizard's victims.
* Beyond the cells lies the summoning chamber.
* In the chamber, the mauled wizard corpse rests serenely on a slab with the tattered destroyed grimoire in his hands.
* The Dark Cursed wand is used to merge the kitchen paper scrap with the grimoire so the incantation becomes readable.
* Reciting the incantation closes the portal and wins the chapter.
* Immediately after victory, the player is sucked into the collapsing portal and reappears before a demonic castle ornamented with stone gargoyles. One appears to move.

=== Restored Incantation (As Recited)

_“By spoon and splatter, by moonlight and batter,  
shut this rude rift in the realm of the natter,  
crumbly-wumbly vex-hex... uh... *mrrrph*.”_

* The player mumbles the ending and assumes it is probably fine.
* The portal closes, but the magic recoil still drags the player through into the next adventure.

== Main Quest Flow

. Speak to the Dark Elf under the Apple Tree to learn how to break the curse.
. Use the Wooden sword to defeat the Bathroom Vampire and obtain the wand handle (first wand half).
. Solve the mirror-shard sunlight setup (Study -> Landing) and defeat the Ballroom Troll.
. Collect the Troll drop (wand tip, second wand half).
. Take silver cutlery from the Ballroom, melt it in the Study forge, then use the bullet mold (with melted silver and _Bullet Making for Dummies_) to create silver bullets.
. Combine gun + silver bullets anywhere to create a Loaded Gun.
. Defeat the Tower Werewolf using the Loaded Gun and take the Dark Mysterious obscure Key.
. Feed the rabbit using Rabbit food found in the yard.
. Meet the now-visible Vegetable Spirit and obtain the missing paper scrap.
. Bring wand parts to the Dark Elf to repair them into the _Dark Cursed wand_.
. Use the Dark Mysterious obscure Key to enter the shed.
. Descend the ominous dark stairway into the dungeon and pass through the bone-filled cells.
. In the summoning chamber, use the _Dark Cursed wand_ to combine the kitchen paper scrap with the tattered grimoire.
. Recite the restored incantation (including an improvised mumbled final word) to close the summoning portal and win the chapter.
. Epilogue hook: the closing portal drags the player to a demonic castle lined with stone gargoyles; one seems to move.
